Onchain Revenue (NearBlocks)

Revenue wallet NEAR inflows from intents.near and buyback wallet outflows parsed from multisig FUNCTION_CALL actions.

• Revenue metric: nearblocks.revenue_near.daily — NEAR transfers from intents.near to 3 revenue wallets

• Buyback outflows: nearblocks.buyback_outflows.daily — Transfers from buybacks.multisignature.near via add_request

• Net revenue: nearblocks.net_revenue_near.daily — Revenue inflows minus buyback outflows

• φ (burn fraction): 0%

— Onchain trace shows buyback wallet balance grew; no transfers to dead address observed

Model: V* = NetIssuance × P₀ × 10,000 / (r × c × b × φ) for burned; without φ for buyback.

Assumptions: Flat price (P_growth=0), constant issuance, 5-year horizon. All outputs are modeled — not predictions.

Key finding: With φ=0 (onchain evidence: buybacks held, not burned), burned breakeven is unreachable. Buyback breakeven requires ~13.5× current volume at current parameters.
